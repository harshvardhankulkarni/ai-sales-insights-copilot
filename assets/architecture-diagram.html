AI Sales Insights Copilot

Data flow from question to answer: numbers, chart, insight. Each step is grounded in the pandas result.

How it runs

  • DATALoad or generate 180 days of synthetic sales.
  • UNDERSTANDClassify the question into one seeded type.
  • ANALYZEPandas computes numbers. No LLM here.
  • CHARTPlotly builds one chart from those numbers.
  • INSIGHTThe LLM turns the numbers into a sentence.
  • ANSWERThe user gets chart, numbers, and insight.
Also runs a weekly auto-insight path: weekly summary to the LLM to a stored narrative.
